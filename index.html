1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with Volt Rush (Android package ID: com.voltrush.game), offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our"). We aim to explain the privacy practices associated with downloading, installing, and playing the game.

2. Introduction

Volt Rush is an Android mobile game distributed through the Google Play Store. This policy describes information that may be collected, used, retained, and shared through the app and integrated services, including Google AdMob. Please review this policy before using the app.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on the app configuration and the services running on your device, information processed may include:

  • Device model, manufacturer, screen characteristics, and operating system version.
  • App version, installation and session information.
  • Device language, region, and approximate location inferred from IP address.
  • IP address and network connectivity information.
  • Google Advertising ID, where available and permitted.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad interactions, and related diagnostics.
  • Gameplay activity and crash or performance data, where these features are implemented.
Important: Some information may be collected directly by third-party SDKs rather than stored by The Data Sequence. Actual collection depends on the SDKs and settings in the installed app version.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Volt Rush, the Android operating system and integrated services may automatically process technical information required for app operation, advertising, fraud prevention, and performance monitoring.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical information may be used to maintain compatibility, diagnose crashes, optimize performance, secure services, and support advertising. We do not intentionally request access to hardware serial numbers or other permanent hardware identifiers.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

Volt Rush stores your progress (unlocked levels, stars and best times, Credits, XP level, owned and equipped kits, headgear, visors and trails, ad-unlock progress, gift and vault progress, your park and player-built courses, daily challenge and streak status, race statistics and settings) locally on your device; it is not sent to us. The game has no accounts, chat or user-generated content sharing.

7. Analytics Information

Usage statistics and diagnostic information may be available through Google Play services or analytics tools that are actually integrated into the game. Such information may be aggregated or associated with pseudonymous identifiers; it should not be assumed to be anonymous.

8. Analytics Services

If a release of Volt Rush includes Firebase Analytics or another analytics SDK, that provider may process app events, device details, and approximate location to support usage reporting. The inclusion of Google AdMob alone does not mean Firebase Analytics is installed. For information about Firebase's practices, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

9. Advertising

Volt Rush includes Google AdMob advertising. It shows a banner ad in its own strip at the bottom of menu and results screens (never during a race and never covering the game), interstitial ads between races (only after a race when you continue, not before level 4, and never within 45 seconds of another full-screen ad), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a stated in-game benefit: tripling the Credits from a race, starting or retrying a race with a one-hit shield, rerolling the daily challenge, doubling park income, unlocking a kit, headgear, visor or trail over several ads, or a bonus on gifts and the Credit Vault. Ad partners may process technical and advertising information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repetition, detect invalid traffic, and personalize advertising where permitted.

10. Google AdMob

Google AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process IP addresses, device characteristics, advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad impressions, clicks, and other data needed to provide and measure advertising. Personalized or non-personalized ads may be shown depending on consent, applicable law, device settings, and ad configuration.

11. Other Advertising Partners

Google AdMob may serve advertisements from Google and other authorized demand partners. If a future version of Volt Rush uses additional standalone ad SDKs or mediation platforms, this policy should be updated to identify those providers accurately. This policy does not assert that Unity Ads or Unity LevelPlay is installed.

12. Advertising Identifiers

On supported Android devices, advertising services may access the Google Advertising ID, a resettable identifier used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, subject to Android settings and applicable requirements. You can manage available advertising controls in your device settings.

13. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain Volt Rush.
  • Display, measure, and support in-game advertising.
  • Investigate technical problems and improve stability.
  • Analyze game performance where analytics are enabled.
  • Detect abuse, fraudulent activity, and security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

14. How We Share Information

Information may be shared with or collected by service providers used to operate the game, including Google AdMob and associated ad technology partners. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and services. We do not claim that information collected by advertising SDKs remains solely on your device.

16. Data Retention

Local gameplay data may remain on your device until it is deleted or the app is uninstalled, depending on device backup settings. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies, configurations, and applicable legal requirements. Contact us for questions about data that we directly control.

17. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we control. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure. Third-party service providers maintain their own security practices.

18. Children's Privacy

Advertising and data processing involving children may be subject to additional legal requirements and Google Play policies. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact us using the details below. We will review applicable requests and take appropriate action. The app's intended audience and advertising configuration must match its Google Play declarations.

19. International Data Processing

Google and other service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Applicable safeguards and rights depend on the provider, location, and relevant privacy laws.

20. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. You can contact us about information we control and use your device or Google privacy controls for information handled by Google.

21. Advertising Choices

You can review advertising privacy options in Android settings and Google account controls. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Volt Rush asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized advertising is used, and you can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy options in the game. Changing personalization preferences does not necessarily remove all ads.

Learn more through Google My Ad Center.

22. Analytics Choices

If optional analytics services are installed, available controls depend on their implementation. You can contact us to ask which analytics tools are used in the current version and what choices are available.

23. Account/Data Deletion Requests

Volt Rush does not necessarily require a player account. If you want to request deletion of personal information held directly by us, contact us using the contact information below. Deleting the app may remove locally stored game data but does not automatically delete information already processed by advertising partners. Requests concerning Google-controlled information may also need to be submitted through Google's privacy tools.

24. Third-Party Links and Services

Volt Rush or its advertisements may link to third-party websites, applications, or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those independent services. Review their policies before providing information.

25. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, SDK integrations, legal requirements, or data practices change. The revised policy will be published at the applicable privacy policy page with an updated date.
